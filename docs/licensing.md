# Licensing Policy

Licenses in this workspace are package-specific. No blanket root license currently governs every package.

Before a package is published, it must include:

- an explicit SPDX license field in its manifest;
- package-local license text;
- required notices for copied or derived code;
- a completed source-origin and provenance review.

Wayforge's preferred license is Apache-2.0, but the final decision remains pending its provenance review. Wayforge must remain private until that review and its package-local licensing metadata are complete.

Future packages may use different licenses when their manifests, distributed files, and notices make the distinction unambiguous.
